Our story

Kelvin's Tech Blog is a learning-focused technology blog created to explain technical ideas in clear language and encourage practical learning.

Technology can feel difficult when you are starting out. This blog breaks large topics into smaller lessons and encourages readers to practise what they learn.

Our principle is simple: learn a concept, practise it, and use it to solve a problem.

What we value

  • Clear explanations for beginners.
  • Hands-on practice and project-based learning.
  • Responsible and accessible use of technology.
  • Continuous improvement and knowledge sharing.

Areas of focus

Web development
Creating websites and web applications, starting with HTML foundations.
Data analysis
Using spreadsheets, SQL, and Python to explore data and communicate findings.
AI and automation
Understanding practical ways to connect tools and automate repetitive tasks.
